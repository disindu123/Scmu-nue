01MEDIA UNIT
STORYTELLING

We connect people, events and ideas through visual media.

The Media Unit of Sanghabodhi College, Nittambuwa supports the school community through event coverage, photography, videography, live broadcasting, editing, graphic design and digital communication.

Our work is student-driven and collaborative — giving young creators space to learn, lead, experiment and build professional media skills while celebrating the identity of our school.
